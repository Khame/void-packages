UReSample source availability
=============================

This Double Commander package includes src/uresample.pas (UReSample).
Its license requires informing end users that the source is available at no
extra charge. The complete Double Commander 1.2.9 source archive is available
at:

https://github.com/doublecmd/doublecmd/releases/download/v1.2.9/doublecmd-1.2.9-src.tar.gz
